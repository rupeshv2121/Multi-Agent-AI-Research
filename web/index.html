The crew

    
Four specialists, one hand-off chain

    
Each agent does one job well and passes its output down the line — so a weak search can't quietly become a confident report.


    

      

        01
        
Scout

        
Queries the live web through Tavily and returns titles, URLs and snippets from reliable, recent sources.

        web_search
      

      

        02
        
Reader

        
Opens the most promising links, strips the page chrome and pulls out the text that actually matters.

        scrape_url
      

      

        03
        
Writer

        
Synthesises everything into an introduction, at least three explained findings, a conclusion and sources.

        llama-3.3-70b
      

      

        04
        
Critic

        
Grades the report out of ten, names its strengths, flags what's thin, and delivers a one-line verdict.

        llama-3.3-70b
      

    

  


  
  

    
Under the hood

    
How a run unfolds


    

      
1
You name a topic
Anything from a broad field to a pointed question. The topic is passed straight to the search agent.

      
2
Agents run in sequence
Each step streams its status back to this page over server-sent events, so you watch it happen live.

      
3
You get a sourced report
Read it here, check the critic's score, follow the sources, or download the whole thing as Markdown.
